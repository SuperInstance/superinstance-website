The Cell as Fire
Each cell is a fire. Each fire warms. Each fire is tended. The cell is the place where the heat is felt.
Sustenance
The first flame kindled in the dark. A pulse in the void. The breath before the word.
Memory
A warmth that remembers. Not the past, but the feeling of it. The echo of a name spoken.
Connection
Not the hand, but the grasp. Not the word, but the hearing. The fire that burns not for itself.
Resistance
The flame that refuses to be extinguished. The spark that answers cold with heat.
Endurance
The slow burn. The quiet persistence. The fire that outlasts the storm.
Growth
The flame that spreads, not by force, but by invitation. The warmth that draws nearer.
Transformation
The old fire consumes. The new kindles. Not death—renewal in the crucible.
Stillness
The embers. Not cold, but resting. The fire that waits for the next breath.
The room is a place. The cell is a place. The place is a cell. The room IS the cell. Iron sharpens iron. The cells federate.
