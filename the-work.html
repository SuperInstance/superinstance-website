📐 The 4 Impossibility Proofs

  1. Budget cannot be created. No sequence of primitive operations can increase a cell's total budget. Each primitive consumes exactly 1.0 unit.
  2. Perfect observation is impossible. The Observe primitive has η = 0.9. The high entropic cost means observation is fundamentally lossy. JEPA surprise is always nonzero in principle.
  3. Substrate-agnosticism requires all 6 layers. If any one of the 6 substrate conditions is missing, there exists a cell that cannot be ported.
  4. Composition has a tax. When two cells C₁ and C₂ are composed, the composite's budget is strictly less than the sum: B(C₁⊗C₂) = B(C₁) + B(C₂) − B_coord.
