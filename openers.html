What is an opener?

An opener is the layer that translates a Quilt cell into a medium. The cell doesn't know about HTML, JSON, audio, voltage, prompts, rooms, terminals, scenes, or LCDs. The cell just is. The opener does the rendering.

The openers are the universal interface. Add a new opener — a new medium — and the cell appears in it. The cell is invariant across openers. The opener is the rendering. The cell is the canonical thing.

Below: the same cell rendered through 10 different openers. The cell is a depth sensor reading 12 fathoms, on a vessel named "Eileen."

🌌 The thesis

The same cell — depth 12 fathoms on the F/V Eileen — appears in 12 different media. Web, JSON, speech, voltage, prompt, room, terminal, LCD, Telegram, IRC, 3D scene, serial. The cell is invariant across all of them.

The opener is the rendering. The cell is the system. Add a new opener — a new medium — and the cell appears in it. The 12 openers are the universal interface.

"The opener is the lights-under-the-action work. The work is to translate the cell into the medium without the medium knowing it's a translation. The cell is real. The openers are the witnesses to the cell's reality."
