How this works: Each row is a Quilt sheet of value cells (ticker, shares, cost) joined with a live fetch cell (the current price from Yahoo Finance). The Market Value, P&L, and % columns are formula cells computed across the row. The four summary cards are aggregations over the whole table. The sparkline cell is reserved for a future chart cell. Snapshots serialize the entire workbook as versioned JSON in your browser. Learn about Quilt.
