FAQ

When does Hosted Quilt launch?
Free and Pro tiers are slated for Q1 2026. Team is Q4 2026. We'll email all backers the moment we're ready. The free tier banner is a soft launch — we already run a hosted instance at quilt.superinstance.dev with all 18 repos, so the infrastructure is real and tested.
Why are the Pro features worth $9/month?
Two reasons. (1) Your work persists in the cloud, not just your browser — you can switch devices and keep going. (2) Federation: connect to your own ESP32 sensors, Jetson robots, Codespace runtimes. The cell graph is one runtime across your fleet. That second one is genuinely hard to build yourself. The R2 canonical store + MQTT broker + custom domain would cost you ~$30/month on Cloudflare + a couple weekends of glue code.
Can I self-host?
Yes. Every repo is Apache 2.0. The hosted service is convenience — the same features (federation, R2 store, MQTT) work on your own infrastructure. See the Quilt ecosystem for the 18 repos and how to assemble them yourself.
What about the cloud costs?
Until the hosted service is live, the cloud bills (Cloudflare R2 + Workers + Pages) come out of pocket. The Buy Me a Coffee and GitHub Sponsors links help cover those. Eventually we crowd-fund for hardware (an actual fleet of Quilt instances to demonstrate the federation at scale) and a hosted service. If you want to help, every coffee counts.
Is my data private?
On Free, your work stays in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server. On Pro, your sheets are stored encrypted in Cloudflare R2; only you can decrypt them. On Team, you get audit logs and SOC 2 compliance. The architecture is open source — you can audit it yourself.
How does the cell model help AI agents?
Three ways. (1) An agent's working memory is a sheet — you can address any cell by URI, audit any cell's history, replay the agent's session deterministically. (2) Federation — your agent can subscribe to a sensor on a boat, a model on Cloudflare, a user prompt in your terminal, all via the same quilt:// URI. (3) Self-improvement: quilt-evolve uses RLAIF on a sheet of agent outputs, regenerating the lowest-scoring cells. The cell model makes the agent's state first-class, debuggable, and federated.
