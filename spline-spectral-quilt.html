📜 The Theorem

1. B-spline basis functions are eigenvectors of the path graph Laplacian. λk = 2(1 - cos(kπ/n))

2. The Cox-de Boor recurrence is Fibonacci for function spaces. Each level builds on the two previous levels.

3. Spline smoothing and spectral filtering solve the same variational problem: min fTL f subject to fitting constraints.

"The B-spline basis doesn't just look smooth — it is the eigenbasis of a diffusion operator."

〰️ The structure

The path graph is the simplest non-trivial graph: 5 nodes, 4 edges, like a string of pearls. The Laplacian L = D - A is the discrete second derivative. Its eigenvalues are the frequencies.

📊 Eigenvalues

🧮 Cox-de Boor

B_{i,0}(t) = 1 if t_i ≤ t < t_{i+1}, else 0

B_{i,p}(t) = (t - t_i) / (t_{i+p} - t_i) · B_{i,p-1}(t)
         + (t_{i+p+1} - t) / (t_{i+p+1} - t_{i+1}) · B_{i+1,p-1}(t)

🔁 The Fibonacci

The Cox-de Boor recurrence at each level p depends on the two previous levels p-1 and p-2. This is the Fibonacci recurrence, but for function spaces instead of numbers. 

Fibonacci:    F(n) = F(n-1) + F(n-2)
Cox-de Boor:  B_p = c_1 · B_{p-1} + c_2 · B_{p-2}

Same structure, different object.
