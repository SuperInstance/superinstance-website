A fleet, not a product

The substrate that evolves its own inference — and shows the receipts.

SuperInstance is an open ecosystem of kernels, instruments, and worlds: a 6-opcode cell kernel that runs identically in TypeScript, Rust, C, and WASM; hash-chained receipts that make every claimed number verifiable; hardware-conscious tools for people who install, instrument, and reason about machines; and playable cities, floors, and oceans built on top. Most of what you'll find here runs in your browser, right now, for free.
